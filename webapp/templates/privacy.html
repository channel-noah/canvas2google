Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Canvas2Google copies your Canvas assignments into Google Tasks and your Canvas calendar events into a Google Calendar named “Canvas”. This page explains what it stores and why.

What we store

  • Your Google account email and ID, to recognize you when you sign in.
  • Your Canvas calendar feed link, encrypted, so the app can read your Canvas calendar.
  • Google access tokens, encrypted, so the app can update your “Canvas” calendar and tasks while you're away.
  • Sync records: which Canvas item matches which Google event or task, plus a short history of recent syncs (including assignment titles).
  • Your preferences: time zone and whether automatic sync is on.

What we access in Google

  • Google Calendar: only calendars this app creates. It can't see or change your other calendars.
  • Google Tasks: the app creates and updates tasks in a list named “Canvas”.

Canvas2Google's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your data is used only to provide the sync. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

How it's protected

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Feed links and Google tokens are encrypted in the database with a key stored separately from it.
  • Sign-in is through Google; this site never sees your Google password.

Deleting your data

  • Signed in, go to Settings → Delete my account. That removes everything listed above and revokes this app's Google access. Events and tasks already copied to your Google account stay there; delete the “Canvas” calendar and task list in Google if you don't want them.
  • You can also remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions? Use the support email shown on Google's sign-in consent screen for Canvas2Google.
